# After Hours audio provenance

The 24-bar, 40-second three-beat score and the original musical cue arrangements were newly composed for this work-in-progress. They were rendered using `tools/compose_after_hours.py`; the script pitch-shifts, mixes, pans and adds short room reflections. That earlier score remains in the source as a preserved reference and is no longer the background music in the 0.2.3 test. The sling, wood and glass cues are now reworked using the recordings below; `tools/render_character_sounds.py` creates those mixes, the character cues and a short synthesized iron impact. Source samples are included in `tools/samples`, and their original paths and verified Git blob SHA-1 hashes are in `tools/samples/SAMPLE_MANIFEST.json`. Music quality, balance, and looping still require listening on an Android phone; technical measurements are not a listening verdict.

- **Folk harp and strumstick recordings:** Versilian Community Sample Library (VCSL), Versilian Studios LLC, https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL, Creative Commons Zero 1.0, https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/ . The recorded pitches mapped in the script follow the measured pitch conventions recorded in the Mothlight source notes.
- **Cello and violin-section recordings:** Versilian Studios / VSCO 2 Community Edition, CC0 1.0, obtained as shortened mono sample derivatives from `lidge-ai/music2-gen` (`instruments/strings/samples`), with exact paths and original-file hashes in the sample manifest. The recordings are further transposed and mixed in this game. Source and notices: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE and https://github.com/lidge-ai/music2-gen/blob/main/THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md .
- **Interface cues:** `Sounds/click-b.ogg`, `Sounds/switch-a.ogg`, and `Sounds/tap-a.ogg` from David's user-compiled Kenney collection. The collection includes Kenney CC0 notices; because original pack boundaries were merged, the individual underlying pack identity cannot be reconstructed reliably. Respectful attribution: Kenney, https://kenney.nl/assets . The input ZIP remains separate and unchanged.

Interface source SHA-256: click-b `154991a6d9ac80ae193ebad3de7eacb642821e9e48d801d054aa9d2b1004e8c6`; switch-a `1a4c90fed704b4f4b36d58f481117cfac5cdc25279a9af97d4765b71ff21ed48`; tap-a `f74c1334c0c1ab9ec69f3dd723611a8eb89a2630df6c3760dee3b09413370c83`.

- **Reitanna (Freesound), CC0 1.0:** four recorded adult female voice reactions, edited for short gargoyle hit and idle sounds: [ooh](https://freesound.org/people/Reitanna/sounds/323707/), [big gasp](https://freesound.org/people/Reitanna/sounds/343878/), [laugh 87](https://freesound.org/people/Reitanna/sounds/343944/), and [giggle 8](https://freesound.org/people/Reitanna/sounds/343991/).

- **micahlg, CC0 1.0:** [wood impact](https://freesound.org/people/micahlg/sounds/421929/) layered with the existing cello cue.
- **mccormick_iain, CC0 1.0:** [glass break](https://freesound.org/people/mccormick_iain/sounds/371091/) layered with the original harp cue.
- **Iron:** newly synthesized decaying metallic tones layered with the above CC0 wood-impact recording.

The actual source recordings and original earlier cue renders are retained in `tools/samples/sfx/`. `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json` records each original Freesound link, mirrored source Git blob ID and SHA-256; all listed recordings are CC0 1.0. Modified cues are rendered to short mono 32 kHz Ogg Vorbis, with fades, filtering and level adjustment. Cues and music remain independently muteable. The files have been decoded and event routing tested locally, but balance and character suitability still need a human listening test on the BLU.

## Level music — 0.2.3

`tools/compose_evening_scores.py` renders an original menu theme and nine distinct, related 16-bar level themes. The score uses rounded triangle-like leads, sine-based bass and restrained soft chords, with new written note phrases and harmonies. The sound palette is informed by the actual locally synthesized Lanternwake 0.4.0 soundtrack; no Lanternwake audio or note sequences were copied. Every piece has been decoded and checked for level, length and loop-boundary discontinuities. The game crossfades the score when the night changes and retains independent music/SFX settings. The new music and its balance have not yet been assessed by a listener on David's phone; Desi and David's listening verdict is the deciding test.

## Family asset audio — 0.2.4 private test

David supplied licensed music and effects and asked for them to be used in the game. Desi preferred the 0.2.3 music on Nights 5–9; those five tracks and the gargoyle voices are unchanged in the private test. Four earlier nights use edited and level-matched Tomality music, selected using the creator's fantasy/electro-swing genre descriptions and signal measurements; suitability and phone-speaker balance still require David and Desi's listening. Music: Tomality, Free Game Music Sampler, https://tomality.com/ and https://tomality.itch.io/free-sampler-pack-1. Night 1 uses `Foreword LOOP 1`; Nights 2, 3 and 4 use `Space Cat LOOP 2`, `LOOP 4` and `LOOP 3`, respectively. These are Tomality's music, edited for level, treble and a short tail-to-head loop blend; they are not new original compositions by this project.

The heart launch layers `Whoosh 1-1.wav`, the wood impact layers `Wood 14-8.wav`, and the iron impact layers `Shield Metal Impact 1-3.wav` from Place Holder Assets' 50 Free Game Sounds Pack (https://placeholder-assets.itch.io/50-free-sounds-pack), trimmed and mixed with the existing cues. No gargoyle recordings were replaced. The help screen credits both creators when the private assets are installed.

The two source ZIPs live in the private asset collection, not this public repository. Both licenses allow use and edits within projects while restricting distribution as standalone asset packs. `tools/build_private_audio_overlay.py` validates the supplied archives and builds only the selected game assets; `tools/apply_private_apk_overlay.py` inserts them into the version 8 Android build prior to signing. The public CI APK lacks the private overlay and is not the family listening build. Save the final signed APK privately. The exact mix, musical fit, loop transitions, upgrade and audio playback need a real device and human ears; passing decoding and routing tests does not establish these.
