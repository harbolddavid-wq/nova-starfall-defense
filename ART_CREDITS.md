# After Hours original artwork and prompts

The following original project assets were generated using the built-in image-generation tool, then resized and encoded as WebP for in-game use. The original generated PNGs are retained separately by the image-generation workflow. The references below were used for cohesive visual style, not as artwork substituted for actual game rendering.

## Masquerade opponent — `app/src/main/assets/art/masked-phantom.webp` (0.4.0)

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the two existing opponents as visual references, then resized with transparency preserved. The in-game floating, flinch, idle and exit motion is implemented by the canvas renderer. Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent full-body 2D game enemy sprite. Input images: both existing characters are style references only, not designs to copy. Create ONE new adult female masquerade phantom for the same mischievous gothic slingshot game: an elegant floating moonlit enchantress with a half-mask lifted beside her expressive face, long flowing dark violet hair, an intricate plum-and-silver cabaret costume and wispy veil-like sleeves. Her confident, playful expression and floating dancer silhouette must look very different from the crouching stone gargoyle and the standing bat-winged rival. Attractive, tasteful, fully clothed and non-explicit. Three-quarter view facing left, head and feet fully inside the image; compact readable silhouette at tiny game scale, separate arms and clear facial expression. Polished hand-painted 2D animation-film finish, violet, burgundy, antique silver and subtle moonlight matching the references. Truly transparent background, no floor or scenery, no ground shadow, no text, letters, logo, watermark, interface or frame.

## Shield glint — `app/src/main/assets/art/shield-glint.png` (0.4.0)

A resized 128×128 copy of `Particles/Complex/star/star_9.png` from David's Kronbits 1000 Free Game Assets particle pack. The original 512×512 source SHA-256 is `c53275c57047abdfdba62086fb1b5eb23eded4e22c98dd86234cb96d724e55ea`; the pack is CC0 per its source listing, https://kronbits.itch.io/particle-pack. The texture is actually animated for shield-break glints and subtle chapter atmosphere in game; the original archive stays in the shared asset collection.

## Midnight rival — `app/src/main/assets/art/midnight-rival.webp` (0.3.0)

Generated with the built-in image-generation tool using the existing gargoyle and Vesper art as visual references, then resized with transparency preserved for gameplay. Prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent-background character sprite for a polished gothic slingshot game. Reference image 1 shows the existing winged gargoyle opponent; reference image 2 shows the heroine and the established painterly art direction. Create ONE entirely NEW, unmistakably different adult female gargoyle-like rival, a charismatic midnight cabaret enchantress with a confident mischievous expression, an elegant plum and black costume, jewelry and small sculptural wings. Attractive and flirtatious, tasteful and non-explicit. Full body with head, wings and feet fully inside the frame; three-quarter view in a dynamic poised stance, facing left. Strong readable silhouette suitable for a small game sprite. Rich painted textures, expressive face, refined silhouette, warm gold rimlight and violet burgundy palette consistent with the reference; avoid copying the existing character's pose, face or outfit. Genuinely transparent background. No scenery, no platform, no shadow on a ground plane, no border, no letters, no logos, no watermarks.

## Vesper — `app/src/main/assets/art/vesper-after-hours.webp`

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a single transparent-background character sprite for an original side-view 2D gothic fantasy slingshot game, Velvet Fling: After Hours. Create Vesper, a clearly adult vampire cabaret magician: confident, mischievous and warm, with elegant black-plum hair, expressive face, a tailored deep wine and black stage outfit with gold details, tasteful and fully clothed, and a dramatic short cape. Full body, boots to head, three-quarter view facing right, a graceful ready-to-launch pose with her hands near her waist; elegant readable silhouette, personality and face at small game scale. Polished hand-painted 2D animation-film quality, cohesive dark burgundy, muted gold and violet palette with warm rim light, softly modeled but not photorealistic. Isolated sprite only on genuine transparent background, no floor, no props in front of her, no other characters, no letters, no borders, no UI, no watermark. Leave enough clear space around silhouette; no cut off limbs.

## Gargoyle — `app/src/main/assets/art/gargoyle-after-hours.webp`

Isolated, complete full character with wings and feet, genuine transparent background; no scenery, no objects in foreground, no other characters, no letters, no UI, no borders, no watermark.

## Terrace — `app/src/main/assets/art/terrace-after-hours.webp`

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide landscape environment background for an original 2D side-view gothic slingshot game, Velvet Fling: After Hours. Use the supplied character images ONLY as references for the cohesive hand-painted finish, muted wine, plum, slate-violet and antique-gold color palette, and warm rim lighting. Create an inviting moonlit castle terrace / open-air cabaret courtyard with a deep violet night sky, distant gothic towers, a luminous moon, subtle arched windows and velvet-draped architecture, with a low level stone parapet and warm lantern light. The middle and right foreground must be uncluttered and softly contrasted so animated game characters, blocks and targets remain easy to read on top. Wide side-scrolling stage view, roughly 16:9, camera straight on, polished 2D game background, atmosphere with depth and a restrained magical sparkle. No characters, no props in the foreground, no text, no interface, no watermark, no grid, no logos.
