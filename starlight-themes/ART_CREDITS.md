# Starlight Themes — visual provenance

`assets/wallpapers/moonflower.png` and `assets/wallpapers/conservatory.png` are original wallpapers generated with the built-in image-generation tool for Desi's launcher. The source images and their private in-app copies are retained separately from this public source branch.

Moonflower prompt: “Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: original portrait wallpaper for Desi's personal Android launcher. Create a beautifully illustrated, dreamy twilight garden with a delicate moth and soft celestial stars: graceful silvery lilac moths hovering near luminous moonflowers at the outer edges, a distant crescent moon and finely drawn constellations above, elegant misty foliage along the lower corners. Gentle midnight lavender, dusky rose, pale silver and soft blue; atmospheric, refined hand-painted storybook fantasy, whimsical and serene rather than spooky or dark gothic. Composition: vertical phone wallpaper, calm low-detail open central area so app icons and white labels remain readable, subtly darker behind icons, no hard horizon through the middle. Nothing depicting a device or UI, no text, letters, logos, frames or watermark.”

Conservatory prompt: “Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: second original portrait wallpaper for a personal Android launcher. A dreamy luminous celestial artist's conservatory in the early blue hour, with pale blue and sage foliage, delicate star-shaped flowers, a few small moths, hanging glass prisms and a far-away silver crescent. Airy watercolor and refined hand-painted storybook textures, soft muted teal, periwinkle, pearl and warm ivory; elegant and whimsical. Composition: vertical phone wallpaper, airy dark-mid-tone open center with very restrained detail behind app icons, artwork around the outer edges and bottom; sophisticated and legible, not photorealistic, no phone or screen, no UI, no text, logo, frame or watermark.”

`assets/ambient/night-sky.png` is `Layers/Starfall_Void/00_sky.png` from the user's **Nightscape Parallax Layers - Original** archive. It is original procedural artwork intended for unrestricted reuse and modification in David's personal apps and games. A very faint, optional slow pulse appears over the home background, and respects disabled system animations. The original archive and its `SOURCE_LICENSE_AND_USES.md` are preserved in `/Game Development Assets/2D Art/Nightscape Parallax Layers - Original/`; no source pack is uploaded to the public code repository.

`assets/icons/{midnight,petal}_*.png` and `assets/moth/moth_00.png` through `moth_15.png` are selected actual files from the **Starlight Moonmoth Launcher Kit v1**, original in-house artwork for unrestricted use in David's private family apps and games. The 18 optional icon designs in each palette are selected individually per installed activity; they never change the activity component or placement. The 16 moth frames play once after applying a theme and are skipped if ambient effects or system animation are disabled. Source archive, original editable files, and catalog are in `/Game Development Assets/Icons and UI/Starlight Moonmoth Launcher Kit v1/` and stay private.
